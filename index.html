关于我们About

QuaintAI 是一家专注视觉表示与多模态基础模型的技术公司，研发两样东西：面向多模态基础模型的高 token 压缩率视频编解码器，以及视频原生的多模态统一模型。编解码器输出的 latent representation 同时承担语义对齐，使同一套表示可以同时服务视觉理解与视觉生成。公司目前处于技术研发阶段。

QuaintAI is a technology company focused on visual representation and multimodal foundation models. We develop two things: a video codec with a high token compression ratio, built for multimodal foundation models, and a video-native unified multimodal model. The latent representation produced by the codec also carries semantic alignment, so that a single representation can serve both visual understanding and visual generation, and the company is currently at the research and development stage.
