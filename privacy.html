隐私政策Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026-09-20Last updated: 2026-09-20

一、适用范围

本政策说明 QuaintAI（以下称「我们」）在你访问 quaint-ai.com（以下称「本网站」）或通过邮件与我们联系时，如何处理相关个人信息。本政策仅适用于本网站，不适用于任何第三方网站或服务。

二、我们处理哪些信息

本网站是静态信息页面，不设注册、登录或订阅功能，不使用 cookie，也不部署分析、广告或追踪脚本。涉及的信息仅限以下三类：

  • 来信信息：当你通过 contact@quaint-ai.com 与我们联系时，我们会收到你在邮件中提供的信息，例如邮箱地址、称呼与邮件正文内容。
  • 访问日志：本网站由第三方静态网站托管服务承载，该服务可能按其通行做法记录服务器访问日志（例如 IP 地址、访问时间、浏览器标识）。我们不对这些日志进行分析或用户画像。
  • 本地语言偏好：为记住你选择的界面语言，本网站会在你的浏览器本地存储中写入一项语言偏好。该数据仅保存在你的设备上，不会发送给我们，你可随时在浏览器中清除。

三、使用目的

上述信息仅用于阅读、回复和处理你的来信，以及维持本网站的正常运行与安全。我们不将其用于广告投放，也不用于对个人产生法律效果的自动化决策。

四、共享与披露

我们不出售、不出租你的个人信息。仅在下列范围内可能涉及第三方：为提供邮件与网站托管服务所必需的范围内；以及法律法规要求或主管机关依法要求披露时。

五、保存期限

来信及相关往来记录，仅在处理你的请求以及满足法定保存要求所必需的期间内保存，此后我们会删除或作匿名化处理。

六、你的权利

你可以来信要求访问、更正或删除我们持有的与你相关的个人信息，或撤回你此前作出的同意。我们会在适用法律规定的期限内答复。

七、未成年人

本网站不面向未成年人，我们也不会主动收集未成年人的个人信息。

八、本政策的变更

我们可能不时更新本政策。更新后的版本自在本页面发布之日起生效，请以页面顶部标注的「最后更新」日期为准。

九、联系方式

如对本政策有任何疑问，请联系 contact@quaint-ai.com。

1. Scope

This policy explains how QuaintAI ("we", "us") handles personal information when you visit quaint-ai.com (the "Site") or contact us by email. It applies to this Site only, and not to any third-party website or service.

2. What information is involved

The Site consists of static information pages. There is no registration, login or subscription; it sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Only the following is involved:

  • Correspondence. If you write to contact@quaint-ai.com, we receive whatever you include in that email, such as your email address, name and message.
  • Server logs. The Site is served by a third-party static hosting provider, which may record standard server access logs (for example IP address, timestamp and user agent). We do not analyse these logs or use them to profile visitors.
  • Local language preference. To remember the interface language you choose, the Site stores a language preference in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can clear it in your browser at any time.

3. How we use it

We use this information solely to read, answer and act on your message, and to keep the Site running and secure. We do not use it for advertising, or for automated decision-making that produces legal effects concerning you.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent your personal information. Third parties are involved only to the extent necessary to provide email and website hosting, and where disclosure is required by law or by a competent authority acting lawfully.

5. Retention

Correspondence is retained only for as long as needed to handle your request and to meet applicable statutory retention requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

6. Your rights

You may write to us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, or to withdraw consent you previously gave. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

7. Minors

The Site is not directed at minors, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. An updated version takes effect when it is published on this page; the "last updated" date is shown at the top.

9. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact contact@quaint-ai.com.
